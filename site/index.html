Questions

Does my PC need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

It needs to be on the same network as your Apple device. Wi-Fi and Ethernet on the same router is fine. Guest networks and routers with "client isolation" block AirPlay.

Which devices work?

iPhone, iPad and Mac with Screen Mirroring, plus any app that plays AirPlay audio (Music, Spotify, podcasts and so on).

Does it mirror in 4K?

AirGlass asks your device for 1080p. Phones can't mirror 4K landscape smoothly (they drop to about 28 fps), and 1080p gives about 60 fps. Full screen is upscaled with a high-quality filter.

Can I AirPlay a YouTube or Netflix video?

Use Screen Mirroring. AirGlass doesn't support the "cast a video link" kind of AirPlay that some video apps use, and many of those apps block it on non-Apple receivers anyway.

Is there a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time $2.99 that you can use on up to 3 of your PCs.

Is it open source?

Yes. AirGlass is licensed under the GPL-3.0 and the full source code comes with every download. Buying Pro supports development and gets you the ready-made installer, updates and support.

Windows warns me about the installer. Is it safe?

New apps from small developers can trigger a SmartScreen warning until they build up reputation. Click More info → Run anyway. The installer doesn't need administrator rights except to add one firewall rule so your devices can find the PC.
