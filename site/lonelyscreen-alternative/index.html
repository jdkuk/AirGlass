Where AirGlass is a better fit

No recurring cost

LonelyScreen is billed yearly. AirGlass's free edition has no time limit and no fee at all; if you want the floating window, recording, drawing or OBS mode, Pro is a single $9.99 payment that covers up to 3 PCs, not a subscription.

Open source

AirGlass's full source code is on GitHub under the GPL-3.0 licence, so anyone can read exactly what it does. LonelyScreen is closed source.

No account, no analytics

AirGlass needs no account and sends no analytics; it's a single native app with its own Bonjour-equivalent responder, so nothing but the mirrored picture and sound leaves your network.

Where LonelyScreen might fit better

If you specifically need LonelyScreen's own feature set or already have a paid licence that's working for you, there's no need to switch. This page is about choosing a receiver, not about any fault with LonelyScreen; both need the phone and PC on the same network to work at all.

Questions

Is there a free alternative to LonelyScreen?

Yes. AirGlass mirrors iPhone, iPad and Mac screens to Windows for free, with no watermark, no time limit and no yearly fee. LonelyScreen's $19.95-a-year personal licence follows a free trial download.

Is AirGlass open source?

Yes, under the GPL-3.0 licence; the full source is on GitHub. LonelyScreen is closed source.

Does AirGlass play AirPlay music like LonelyScreen?

Yes. AirGlass receives AirPlay music as Apple Lossless (16-bit, 44.1 kHz) and plays it bit-for-bit through the PC's speakers or DAC.

What does AirGlass Pro add over the free edition?

A floating, resizable glass window; screenshots; MP4 recording with sound; drawing on screen; region zoom; OBS mode; two devices mirroring at once; and a PIN or allow-list for new devices. It's $9.99 once, for up to 3 PCs.
