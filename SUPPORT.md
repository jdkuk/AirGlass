# Getting help

1. **Read [Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md).** It covers almost every "my PC doesn't show up" case.
2. **Questions and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/jdkuk/AirGlass/discussions).
3. **Bugs:** [open a bug report](https://github.com/jdkuk/AirGlass/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with your log.
4. **Purchases, refunds, license keys:** reply to your Lemon Squeezy receipt email. Refunds are available within 14 days if you've used Pro for fewer than 10 mirroring sessions (the AirGlass tray menu shows your count).
